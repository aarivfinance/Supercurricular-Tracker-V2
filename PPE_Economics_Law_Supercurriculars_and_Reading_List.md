## Personal statement pairing note

You mentioned the structure *"I read/did X, then did Y, because Z."* The tables above are set up so you can pair a **mainstream** book with a **niche** one on the same underlying debate — Rawls↔Nozick, Piketty↔Hayek, Hart↔Dworkin — and then link that to an actual competition where you tested the idea, e.g.:

> "Reading Rawls' *A Theory of Justice* led me to question whether the 'veil of ignorance' holds up against Nozick's entitlement theory, which I explored in my Sidgwick Prize essay on [your question], arguing that..."

That contrast — not just volume of reading — is what shows real engagement rather than a checklist.
